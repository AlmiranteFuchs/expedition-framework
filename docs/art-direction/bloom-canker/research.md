# Colony source reference study

The user rejected the pale gall / split rock-like knot concepts and requested
more research. This is a set of design possibilities, not implementation or canon.
The user subsequently selected 3 or 4 for implementation. Alpha.17 implements a low Shutter Bloom combining 4's staggered opening with 3's broad layered plates. See `../../shutter-bloom.md`.

## What the references actually support

- [Mojang: The Garden Awakens, Java 1.21.4](https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/32385811139085-Minecraft-Java-Edition-1-21-4-The-Garden-Awakens):
  eyeblossoms communicate their opening/closing state; transitions have sound and
  particles. The Creaking links a visible threat to a hidden environmental source.
  **Design inference:** coordinated behavior can carry the hive identity even
  when individual forms are small. These are references, not a version migration.
- [Australian National Botanic Gardens / CANBR: Banksia](https://cpbr.gov.au/banksia/index.html):
  woody follicles hold protected seeds and open under particular conditions.
  **Borrow:** thick paired valves and contrasting empty chambers. Do not copy
  the fire trigger or imply that the Bloom is a real plant.
- [Kew: fungi photographs](https://www.kew.org/read-and-watch/in-pictures-fungi-spotting-season-at-kew):
  bird's-nest fungi have fluted cups; bracket fungi have shelf-like growth forms.
  **Borrow:** dark interiors, ribbed undersides and growth attached to a host.
  Avoid literal eggs, caps on stalks or dozens of tiny decorative cuboids.
- [North American Mycological Association: lichen basics](https://namyco.org/interests/education/lichen-basics/):
  foliose forms are leafy, squamulose forms have scale-like lobes, and apothecia
  can be cups or discs on a surface. **Borrow:** a shared surface patch carrying
  several small structures, rather than an isolated monument.
- [US National Park Service: slime molds](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/slime-molds.htm):
  describes plasmodial networks functioning as one moving mass and cellular
  aggregation via chemical signals. **Borrow:** distributed coordination and
  signals converging through a colony, not a central anatomical brain/heart.
- [RHS: witches' brooms](https://www.rhs.org.uk/biodiversity/witches-brooms):
  a changed growth pattern can produce dense abnormal branching. Considered but
  deprioritized: a twig bundle risks recreating the wires, vines and finger-like
  geometry the user has already rejected.

## Distinct candidate directions

### 1. Husk relay

One compact, irregular woody capsule, mounted on a floor or host face. Three or
four broad pale valves contain a dark amber-lined chamber. They separate by a
small amount during a colony signal, then settle closed with a dry click. No
floating core. The amber belongs to the inner lining, not a luminous crystal.

Best reference: Banksia follicles. Best fit for preserving the current single
breakable anchor and portable specimen loop. Final silhouette should be hollow
and folded, rather than a stepped rock or a perfectly round seed.

### 2. Listening cups

Three low, unequal fluted cups share a patch of the approved flat tissue. Their
insides are dark, with narrow amber rims. A short sequence of clicks/amber flickers
passes between them and nearby infected creatures as the hive changes focus.
The source reads as a small colony participating in a larger mind.

References: lichen apothecia and bird's-nest fungi. No literal eggs or anatomical
mouths. One model/ownership point can retain the current breakable-anchor rules.

### 3. Folded lamellae

Two or three broad pale fan-shaped plates overlap against a wall or low floor
edge. Their undersides have sparse dark ribs and amber resin only at joins.
Slightly separating plates reveal which colony is active. Thin planes and a
small number of rectangular supporting masses keep it Minecraft-compatible.

Reference: bracket-fungus attachment and gills/lamellae as visual structure.
More attached to the substrate than an upright pod; avoid a generic mushroom.

### 4. Shutter bloom

An angular rosette of four folded bark plates sits low and mostly closed.
During a hive event, one plate opens, then the others, exposing amber inner
surfaces before closing. Nearby source growths answer in sequence. The motion,
not a permanent glow, makes an otherwise quiet object feel aware.

References: foliose/scale-like lichen forms plus Mojang's communicating flowers.
Risk: too symmetrical or flower-like. Use uneven folded plates and no stalk.

### 5. Colony confluence

There is no prominent central organ. Several shallow nodules emerge where the
flat infection meets; amber signals visibly converge on the active node. The
player follows that pattern to locate and sever the source. Harvesting recovers
a small source fragment rather than an entire organism.

Reference: slime-mold organization. Strongest influence-inhabiting-the-land
direction, but needs more interaction/readability design than a model replacement.
It must always give an identifiable, accessible harvest/disable target.

## Shortlist

Husk relay is the strongest single-object redesign with minimal disruption to
the current recovery/engineering loop. Listening cups is the strongest visual
expression of the hive. A future concept comparison should show those as separate
alternatives at actual Minecraft scale, with active/dormant motion keyframes.
Do not merge all reference forms into a busy hybrid.

Keep pale bark / warm gray / charcoal / sparse amber. Keep the approved flat
surface layer, contact-only infection, ordinary vision and existing progression.
New behavior here is a proposal, not an addition to authoritative game mechanics.
