Create a polished VISUAL CONCEPT COMPARISON SHEET for five distinct possible colony-source objects in a Minecraft mod called The Stillness. Landscape composition, five separated panels in a clean readable two-row layout, three above and two below. Every panel uses the same camera angle, lighting and object scale. Dark warm charcoal studio backdrop. Each object sits on a small square swatch of Minecraft stone terrain for scale, with a very thin flat pale infection skin and sparse amber fissures continuing from its base. Clear small labels: 1 HUSK RELAY, 2 LISTENING CUPS, 3 LAYERED FANS, 4 SHUTTER BLOOM, 5 COLONY CONFLUENCE. This is an art-direction sheet, NOT a texture atlas and NOT an illustration to stretch over block faces. Make the subjects look like convincing actual Minecraft models: a small number of chunky rectangular prisms and broad flat planes, hard low-resolution pixel textures, simple readable silhouettes, no highly subdivided voxel sculptures, no smooth realistic curves. Main objects occupy roughly half to three quarters of one block height. Restrained Creaking / pale oak palette: pale cream and warm-gray bark tissue, charcoal recesses, very sparse dark honey-amber resin. Grain should read as WOOD FIBER, not stone. No green, vines, wire roots, thin fingers, hands, anatomical hearts, crystals, floating cores, rocks, altars, metal, neon glow or fire. Keep surrounding infection strictly FLAT and texture-only. Forms must be VISIBLY DIFFERENT from one another and from stepped rocky stumps. 1 HUSK RELAY: One compact asymmetric woody seed capsule inspired by Banksia follicles, built from three broad thick folded pale shell shutters. A shutter is slightly ajar, revealing a narrow dark amber-lined interior. Mounted directly into the substrate; not on a stalk. The shell has large empty spaces and clearly folded edges. A small secondary silhouette shows it closed and cold. 2 LISTENING CUPS: A cluster of three SHORT unequal hollow square-edged cups sharing one low pale tissue patch. Inspired by fluted bird's-nest fungi and lichen apothecia. Broad rims, dark ribbed interiors, tiny amber marks deep inside; NO eggs, eyes, mouths, teeth or tall stems. Cups should feel grown from the same material as the flat surface infection. Show tiny subtle amber signal marks passing between the cups, not laser beams. 3 LAYERED FANS: Three broad thin angular pale fan-shaped shelves overlapping sideways from a low surface attachment. Inspired by bracket fungi, but no conventional mushroom caps or stalks. Deep charcoal ribbing underneath and a little amber resin at the joins. Strong horizontal silhouette with open air between the plates. 4 SHUTTER BLOOM: An uneven low rosette made of four broad folded bark plates, offset and asymmetric, no stem. One plate has lifted, others partially unfolding, revealing dim amber inner surfaces. Make it look like dry bark opening in a mechanical yet natural sequence, not a beautiful flower or a star-shaped crystal. Include a small closed/cold state. 5 COLONY CONFLUENCE: No large standalone central object. Three low flat pale nodules are spread across the terrain swatch, continuous with a flat bleached infection membrane. A few restrained amber texture strokes converge on one slightly open nodule; the effect is an organism inhabiting the ground. The active target remains easy to distinguish. Use ample negative space between panels. The mood is quiet, uncanny, watchful, woody and invasive. Avoid decorative clutter; prioritize forms we could actually model and recognize at Minecraft inventory and world scales.
