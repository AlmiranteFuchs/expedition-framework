# Expedition Framework 0.6.0-alpha.26

Requires Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.x, Java 21 and Patchouli
1.21.1-92-NEOFORGE. Install the Expeditions and Patchouli jars in the mods
folder. Create and dimension mods are optional.

- First Bearing teaches two witness marks and extraction. Ordinary charters
  offer private admission and retain personal reports after extraction.
- A Wayroot can bind a player-built arch of Pale Graft Posts and a Graft
  Collar. Amber fills the opening until the party seals. The pale bark and
  aged copper have dedicated textures.
- Successful expeditions award Return Knots. Brew Return Sap in a Sap Jar,
  move it by bucket, Rootwick or Create pump, and spend it in the Basin.
  The jar can also break down unwanted items by crouch-use or hopper feed.
- The Basin builds weighted material, curiosity and gear pools from the
  recovered dimension's item namespace. A Hard or Brutal extraction opens
  Gear. The ledger shows sample finds and their odds.
- Charter and salvage screens use compact field sheets. Routine cues appear
  above the hotbar, and `/expeditions history` retains detailed run records.
- Resonance machines show their operating state and support comparators.
  The optional simple generators setting offers a gentler power setup.

Existing Wayroots can still open an overhead aperture without a collar.
Tokens still pay charter fees. Back up your world before updating an alpha
build. The in-game test world and its fixtures are not part of the release.
